## Original cache and policy semantics

decision nodes and permission bitmaps inside the same prepaid context inherited
from the real socket. Each class has a distinct birth kind. Fixed records,
64-owner capacity, TCP 96 KiB, DNS 64 KiB and the original backing/metadata
allowance remain unchanged. Exhaustion refuses before publication or generic
allocation; no additional queue, ledger, arena or application executor exists.

The maintained cache keys, sequence checks, 512 buckets, lookup/RCU behavior,
reclamation threshold and permissive-policy update logic remain. A cache hit
does not transfer its creator's charge to another context. Private cache
creation propagates its actual finite allocation/update failure instead of
silently proceeding through an uncharged allocation. Both `sock_has_perm` and

Normal cache revocation, replacement and flush still unlink logically even if

## Publication and retirement

The same original AVC slot lock precedes the pool lock. Private replacement
preflights the new PRIVATE node and old PUBLISHED node before atomically
publishing the new birth and queuing the old continuation. It cannot leave an
orphan publication when the old owner, generation, bucket or continuation
capacity is invalid. Different original contexts keep their own charges.

A fixed 256-bit seen set validates the complete extended-permission graph
before any return. Actual original birth/size/kind and same-context checks
precede dereferencing linked fields. Aliases, cycles, foreign or unknown
interior addresses retain ownership and poison the private path. Partial
allocation cleanup follows actual nonnull pointers; the `used` bitmask can
still be unset when the original allocation fails.

Unpublished failure cleanup requires PRIVATE state. Published nodes can return
only from their queued RCU callback, after their children. No pointer is read
after returning its view. Backing and cursor space are not reused or refunded.
A finite context-close scan snapshots only fixed identities, then takes the
original slot lock and revalidates the generation before deriving an address.
It is neither a grace-period wait nor proof of complete retirement. Genuine
late descendants remain charged and may require subsequent owned maintenance.

The added factory helper include/drain is selected only by this AVC derivation.
Earlier six/twelve/fourteen/fifteen object modes keep their original selection and
retain their historical source attribution.
