and unchanged Flurl.Http4.0.2 / Refit16.4.0 factories. It requires both clients to
report malformed JSON distinctly from transport failure, and to reject oversized,
malformed-framing and truncated responses. Two final domain calls require fresh

no mutation and no remaining connection. The malformed/domain JSON responses
must actually finish writing; an oversized response may be interrupted by its
bounded consumer. Its raw write/close observations are retained rather than
being called completed writes. Nodes and peers must shut down cleanly and reap.
