Use `python tools/run_java_transaction_http_qualification.py --help` from the

## Candidate review and authority

`--prepare-authority-only` stops before candidate policy mutations. Continue only
with the exact retained `--resume-candidate` and `--candidate-digest` selected by
the original review. Preparation, package signing and constructor inspection do
not grant namespace, result-read, provider or dispatch permission.

The real node must authenticate the administrator and tenant, current
publication/deployment, signed companion and application sources, namespace and
incarnation, purpose-specific policies, original native reservation, protected
clock and installed provider. Historical decoder publications provide only their
verified source bytes; they cannot replace the current execution authority.
Every accepted effect remains associated with its original command, attempt,

## Protected restore and physical retirement

Version 2 selects the business store at `dataDirectory/state`; there is no
`state.stateRoot` override. Prepare a fresh installation `dataDirectory` and
separate fresh `checkpointRoot` for the destination. The original physical store,
checkpoint and catalog owners must be retired before another owner opens them.
Copying catalogs does not grant authority or establish protected continuity.

Inspect and restore the same exact snapshot, immutable destination identity and
reviewed window, then require the actual receipt's `destinationIdentity` when
configuring the restored node. Historical state/effect outcomes remain historical;
restoration changes incarnation and leaves recovery paused for explicit review.
Schema migration selects only the installed closed recipe and verified signed
application source. A compatible reader cannot relabel the original schema or
approve migration.

Retain the actual terminal/pending responses, original query views, package and
binary identities, recipient mutation/deduplication observations, and positive
worker, callback, catalog and native-owner retirement. Collector process success,
a timeout, idle counters or dropping a waiter cannot prove physical cleanup or
an external provider outcome.
