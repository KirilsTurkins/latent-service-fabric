# Code of Conduct

LSF welcomes contributors and users with different backgrounds, identities,
abilities and levels of experience. Everyone, including maintainers, must help
keep participation respectful and free from harassment and discrimination.

## Scope

This policy applies to the repository's issues, pull requests, reviews,
Discussions, documentation and website contributions, and other spaces operated
by the project. It also applies when someone explicitly represents LSF in a
public space. Using LSF or contributing to it does not by itself make someone a
project representative.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas, code and evidence on their merits. Explain technical objections
  constructively and accept corrections without personal attacks.
- Welcome questions and different perspectives. Respect people's names,
  identities, accessibility needs, personal boundaries and available time.
- Keep discussions relevant, allow others to participate and follow reasonable
  requests to stop disruptive behavior or unwanted contact.
- Respect privacy and consent when sharing messages, screenshots or other
  information about people.

Technical disagreement, a rejected contribution, a good-faith bug report or a
request for stronger evidence is not by itself misconduct. Apply the same
standards of respectful behavior when giving and receiving criticism.

Unacceptable behavior includes threats, harassment, discriminatory insults,
sexual harassment or unwanted sexual attention, targeted personal attacks,
publishing private information without consent, impersonation and repeated
disruption or unwanted contact. Retaliation against someone for raising a
concern in good faith or participating in a conduct review is also unacceptable,
even if the review does not establish a violation. Do not evade moderation
restrictions through other accounts or channels.

## Reporting

For abuse on GitHub, use **Report content** or **Report abuse**, or the
[GitHub Support abuse form](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).
See [GitHub's reporting instructions](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
for the available controls. These reports go to GitHub for enforcement of its
platform policies; they are not a private project inbox. This route is also
available if the concern involves the repository owner.

The project does not currently publish a dedicated private conduct contact.
If you already have a private way to contact the
[repository owner, @KirilsTurkins](https://github.com/KirilsTurkins), you may use
it for a project conduct concern. For a moderation request about an existing
public thread, you may ask the owner to review it if you are comfortable doing
so, without reposting abusive material or personal information. Do not open a
public issue or pull request to submit a sensitive conduct report. You do not
need to confront the person involved before reporting.

Provide links, approximate dates, a brief description of what happened and any
immediate safety or contact concerns. Share only the information needed to
understand the incident; avoid copying private correspondence, credentials or
unrelated personal details into public threads.

Report suspected software vulnerabilities through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
Private security advisories are for vulnerability disclosure, not interpersonal
conduct reports.

## Review and enforcement

The repository owner is responsible for project moderation. Maintainers handling
a concern must consider the available context, severity, repetition and safety
of those affected, and apply this policy consistently, including to their own
behavior. They may seek clarification from the people involved. Private report
information must be shared only as needed to assess and address the concern;
absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.

Depending on the behavior, maintainers may:

- Explain the concern, request a correction or issue a warning.
- Remove harmful content, close or lock a disruptive conversation, or restrict
  participation in that discussion.
- Temporarily or permanently restrict project participation within their
  moderation permissions.

Serious threats, harassment or repeated violations may require an immediate
restriction without a prior warning. Where safe and practicable, maintainers
should explain the decision, any duration and what behavior must change without
publishing sensitive report details.

A person affected by a moderation decision may request reconsideration through
the channel used to communicate it, providing corrections or new information.
An involved maintainer should seek an uninvolved maintainer's review where one
is available. The project does not currently have a separate appeals body;
GitHub Support independently handles reports under GitHub's own policies.
